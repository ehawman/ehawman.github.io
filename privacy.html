Privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026

This Google API client is used by rclone, running on the computer of the person who signs in, to read and write that person's own Google Drive files.

  • Data goes directly between your computer and Google. It never passes through or is stored on any server I run, and I never receive it.
  • Google's sign-in token is kept in rclone's settings on your computer.
  • Your data isn't sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

To remove this client's access to your Google account, go to your Google Account's third-party connections.
